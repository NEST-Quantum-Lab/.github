# NEST Quantum Lab

**Global Networking, Efficient Computing & Reliable Sensing for Quantum Information Science**

NEST Quantum Lab at **Kennesaw State University** explores quantum networking and communication, distributed quantum computing, sensing, and artificial intelligence. Led by **Dr. Huayue Gu** in the Department of Information Technology, our research brings together algorithm design, network protocols, optimization, and machine learning to develop efficient and reliable networked systems.

[Research & Publications](https://gghhhyy.github.io/) · [Contact](mailto:hgu2@kennesaw.edu)

## Research directions

- **Quantum networking & communication:** Entanglement distribution, quantum network protocols, and satellite-assisted quantum networks that connect resources across distance.
- **Efficient computing:** Distributed quantum computing, resource allocation, and scheduling for networked computing systems.
- **Reliable sensing:** Quantum sensing and the coordination of distributed sensing resources through communication networks.
- **Intelligence & AI:** Learning-based approaches to network optimization, resource management, and intelligent systems.

Across these directions, we study the tradeoffs among performance, reliability, and resource cost.
